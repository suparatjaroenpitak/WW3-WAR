## Delivery scope

No paid assets, aerial photographs, map screenshots, AI-generated historical reconstructions or third-party
texture images were used. Plain PBR material colours and estimated building heights are procedural visualization choices.
The source data licences above remain applicable to their respective components; the entire package is not CC0.
All download URLs, timestamps and hashes are in `data/metadata/sources.json` and adjacent `.source.json` receipts.
